Wages: Under the new definition of "wages" in the labour codes, certain allowances can be counted, and excluded components are generally limited to 50% of total remuneration. If your "basic + DA" is less than half of your total pay, your gratuity wage base may be higher than your basic. Check with your employer how wages are defined in your payroll.

Eligibility: Permanent employees generally need 5 years of continuous service (exceptions apply on death or disablement). Fixed-term employees directly engaged by the employer are eligible after 1 year of service under the contract, on a pro-rata basis. Service of more than 6 months in the final year is rounded up to a full year.

Scope: The (15 × wages × years) ÷ 26 formula applies to employees covered by the Act or Code. Seasonal establishments and employers outside its scope use different methods (for example, seasonal employees are computed on a different day-rate). Service before 21 November 2025 and the rules of your state may affect your case.

Ceiling: ₹20,00,000, as last notified; this limit can be revised by the government.
